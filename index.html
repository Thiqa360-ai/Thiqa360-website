What THIQA360 Does — and Does Not Do

Operational Boundaries

THIQA360 Does

  • Structures evidence-led verification workflows
  • Supports governed confidence assessment processes
  • Evaluates defined conditions and requirements
  • Produces recommendations for authorised human review
  • Maintains an append-only, tamper-evident evidence log

THIQA360 Does Not

  • Hold funds, financial assets, or collateral
  • Execute payments, transfers, or financial transactions
  • Release assets or value without authorised human sign-off
  • Provide regulatory approval, licensing, or endorsement
  • Replace authorised professional judgment or decision-makers

E.g., what documents, certifications, or approvals must be in place before release?

2 / 5

Are parties and authorised signatories consistently verified?

Do you have documented identity verification and role confirmation for all decision-makers?

Platform Architecture

The Trust Foundation

Four integrated modules working together to structure evidence-led verification and governed release-readiness assessment.

DV

DVI

Party and Entity Verification Confidence. Evaluates the confidence level of party identification and institutional entity verification.

AV

AVI

Asset and Title Evidence Confidence. Structures the verification of asset ownership, title documentation, and evidence completeness.

AS

ASSURA

Conditional Release-Readiness Assessment. Evaluates whether defined conditions appear satisfied, producing a recommendation for authorised human sign-off.

AT

Audit Trail

Append-only, tamper-evident evidence log. Records every verification step, evidence input, condition evaluation, and decision point.
